Har Din · Festival Status App

Your privacy, in plain language.

This policy explains what Har Din does — and does not — do with your information. No legal jargon: if a section doesn't apply to how the app currently works, we say so plainly instead of padding it out.

Overview

Har Din ("we", "our", "the app") is a festival status, greetings, and quotes app. In its current version, the app works entirely offline using content bundled inside the app itself — festival designs, categories, and quotes are stored on your device, not on a server we operate.

That means: no account is required, no personal data is collected by us, and nothing you do inside the app is sent to our servers.

What we collect

Nothing, at present. Har Din does not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or crash-reporting services that transmit data off your device. We don't ask for your name, phone number, or email to use the app.

If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, or notifications, this section will be updated before that version reaches you — and the "Updated" date at the top of this page will change accordingly.

Your photos & text

When you customize a status — adding your name, a photo, or a short message — that content is composed into an image locally on your device. It is not uploaded to us, and we never see it.

The finished image lives in your device's storage (or photo gallery, if you save it there) exactly like any photo you'd take with your camera app.

Sharing

Tapping "Share to WhatsApp" or any other share option hands your created image to that app using your device's native share sheet. From that point on, the image is subject to WhatsApp's (or whichever app you chose) own privacy policy — Har Din is not part of that transfer and doesn't retain a copy.

Permissions

The app may ask for:

  • Photos / storage — so you can add a photo to a status, or save a finished design.
  • Notifications (planned, not yet active) — to let you know about new festival designs.

Each permission is requested only when you use the feature that needs it, and only for that purpose.

Children's privacy

Har Din does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children — since it doesn't collect information from anyone at all in its current form.

Policy changes

As Har Din adds features — accounts, cloud sync, or personalization — this policy will be updated to describe them before they ship. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy. We recommend checking back here occasionally.
